About

A dual
perspective on
communications

From experience in an advertising agency to the creation of Pepite per Tutti, a go-to destination for Milan lifestyle with over 100,000 followers. Starting as an urban trend observer, Ilaria transformed her passion for storytelling into a strategic asset for businesses.
