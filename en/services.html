Focus

Digital PR

I build and manage relationships with the press, creators and influencers to amplify the brand's voice through authentic, authoritative content. Different solutions for different needs.

A

Product Seeding

The most agile solution to generate organic visibility.

Targeted selection of creators and journalists to receive the product, with a curated package that includes a personalized letter, communication materials and key messages. The goal is to generate spontaneous content and editorial mentions.

Includes: contact scouting, press kit preparation, shipping and coverage monitoring.

B

Media Relations & Digital Press Office

For a structured presence in the press and digital media.

Preparation of press releases, digital press kits and comprehensive communication materials. Proactive management of relationships with editorial teams and publications, both online and offline, to secure coverage, interviews and editorial features.

Includes: press releases, custom media list, targeted pitch, follow-up and coverage report.

C

Creator & Influencer Campaign Management

The complete solution for creator and influencer campaigns.

An end-to-end service: from the strategic selection of profiles best aligned with the brand, to negotiation and contract management, through to creative brief preparation, content production coordination and final approval.

Includes: creator scouting and selection, creative brief, negotiation, content management, approvals, performance monitoring and reporting.

D

Integrated Press + Creator Package

Maximum amplification: traditional press and creators working in synergy.

A Digital PR strategy that combines press office and creator campaigns into a single, coordinated plan. Every touchpoint tells the same story with different yet complementary voices, maximizing reach and message credibility.

Includes: all services from packages B and C, with an integrated strategy and unified reporting.
