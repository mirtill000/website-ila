What Pepite per Tutti Is

A magazine born
to observe Milan

Born as a blog in 2011, Pepite per Tutti is today a go-to destination for anyone looking for the best of the city: neighborhood addresses, new openings, recurring formats and a curious eye on what makes Milan different every season.

It isn't a side project to the agency: it's the starting point. This is where Ilaria tests language, formats and strategy every day — before bringing them to the brands she advises.

From the Magazine to Events

From a hashtag
to a ritual

"Pepite a Colazione" was born right here: from breakfast photos shared with the hashtag #pepiteacolazione. The community responded, and that digital ritual grew into a real-life event format hosted in homes across Milan.
