The Story

From a hashtag
to a ritual

It all started with a morning photo: a steaming cappuccino, a freshly baked brioche, light streaming through the window. Andrea and Ilaria — the couple behind Pepite per Tutti — began sharing their breakfasts with the hashtag #pepiteacolazione.

The community responded with enthusiasm, sharing photos of their own breakfasts in return. From there came the inspiration: to transform that digital ritual into something real. And so the themed breakfasts in Milan’s homes were born.

The Concept

Breakfast among strangers

Every month, a different theme. A secret location. A table set with care. And people who don’t know each other, united by the desire to share an authentic experience.

Curated Themes

Each breakfast follows a theme: from French breakfast to Christmas breakfast, from spring to Alice in Wonderland. Menu, decor and atmosphere are designed down to the finest detail.

Secret Locations

Private homes and Milanese residences that change each time. From attic rooms with exposed beams to shabby chic lofts: the address is revealed only to registered guests.

Strangers at the Table

The heart of the format: people who don’t know each other share the same table. No forced networking, just the magic of a well-made breakfast and curiosity about the person next to you.

Sitting down at a table with strangers, welcomed by smiles and embraces, a steaming cup and pastries in abundance.

The Experience

A table that
tells a story

Every detail is carefully thought out: from vintage cups collected at flea markets to tiered stands laden with homemade pastries, from family garden jams made in Umbria to organic yogurt from Fattoria Scaldasole.

Apple crumble with dark chocolate chips, chestnut flour sablés with salted caramel, rustic quiches and spiced juices. This is not a simple brunch: it’s a sensory experience curated like an editorial production.
