The Project

Pepite per
Tutti

Pepite per Tutti was born in 2011 to uncover the treasures of an unexpected Milan: the vintage dairy bar serving the best brioche on the Navigli, the interior design boutique run by two English sisters, the little restaurant just outside the city for a romantic getaway.

Without losing its core concept of discovery and innovation, today the magazine also covers travel guides, shopping, emerging brands, etiquette, home design and food. A 360° magazine that has never lost sight of its original concept.

Audience

Our audience

Our audience is primarily made up of women (25-54) who are curious, well-informed and attentive to the latest trends. Pepite per Tutti is recognized as a space of inspiration, where it is easy to stay up to date on new trends.

But for our followers, Pepite is also a platform where they can seek and ask for advice, share new experiences and identify with a refined and original style.

The Founder

Ilaria Vita

After a degree in communication and a master's in marketing and advertising communication, Ilaria entered the advertising world, working for over 15 years as an account manager on international clients.

Today she manages the Pepite per Tutti communication hub: online magazine, e-commerce, event formats and strategic communication consulting collaborations for brands and companies.
